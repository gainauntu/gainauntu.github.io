Selected Work

Adaptive Edge Anomaly Detection for Smart Water Meters

Independent contract / product development · Jan 2026–Present

A per-meter adaptive baseline for leak detection. Its update rule suppresses or down-weights abnormal observations so that persistent anomalous behavior is not gradually absorbed into what the model treats as normal. After initial experiments with Isolation Forest and autoencoder approaches, the design moved to an adaptive statistical method better suited to household-specific online adaptation and interpretable leak decisions.

Prototyped on an STM32U5 Nucleo platform before migration to custom production hardware. Evaluation on consented household, office, and industrial water-meter data is in progress. Related technical work was incorporated into a patent application filed by the client.

Autonomous Water-Treatment Skimmer · K-water collaboration

Fluton Inc. · 2021–2023

Sole software developer for an autonomous surface platform that runs repeatable cleaning and survey missions with Pixhawk/PX4, RTK-GPS, and LiDAR-based collision avoidance near pool walls and facility structures. AprilTag-guided docking on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano leads into a post-alignment sequence that engages electromagnetic dock retention and wireless charging. During field testing with K-water engineers, the platform completed unattended cleaning, docking, and charging cycles, including multi-hour autonomous operation.

Robotic Pill-Sorting Vision System

Completed contract project · Jan 2026

Visual-recognition pipeline for an initial set of about 16 pill classes from high-resolution front/back image pairs, starting with a CNN classifier and later evaluating DINOv2-based visual representations. A confidence-based rejection rule sends low-confidence predictions to manual handling instead of automatic sorting, because an incorrect pill decision was considered more costly than a rejection. The classifier is integrated with the robotic sorting controller, with a retraining workflow for adding new pill classes.
