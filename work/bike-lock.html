Shared-Bicycle Lock Firmware

Organization
Fluton Inc.
Period
2021–2023
My role
Embedded firmware for the smart lock

At a glance

  • Embedded firmware for a shared-bicycle smart lock mounted at the rear wheel and operated through a mobile app.
  • C/RTOS firmware covering BLE, NFC-related support, GPS, modem communication, sensing and device-state storage.
  • Reduced heap usage in GPS processing and fixed watchdog resets during MCU sleep, for steadier operation in the field.

Outcome

Built the lock-side firmware end to end, from app unlocking over BLE to location, server communication and sensing, and made it steadier for real-world operation by cutting GPS heap usage and fixing watchdog resets during sleep.

The problem

In a shared-bicycle service the lock is the device riders actually touch: it sits at the rear wheel, opens when the app asks, reports its status and location to the service, and runs on a battery for long periods outdoors.

The firmware therefore has to combine secure app interaction, location and connectivity, sensing and power management on one constrained, battery-powered device. Hardware models and service details are not described here; the illustrations are conceptual.

What I did

  • Led the embedded firmware implementation for the smart-lock system.
  • Developed the lock-side embedded logic and the BLE firmware, including the app-connected unlock and detach behaviour.
  • Implemented core device-side communication: BLE, NFC-related payment support, GPS location and modem communication with the server.
  • Implemented sensing and monitoring of battery, humidity, temperature, and tilt and movement.
  • Handled data and state storage and memory management on the device.
  • Improved reliability: reduced heap usage in GPS processing and fixed watchdog resets during MCU sleep.
