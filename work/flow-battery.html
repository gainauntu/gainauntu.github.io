Flow-Battery Monitoring and Protection

Organization
Approtec Inc.
Period
2023–2024
My role
Monitoring, protection and test automation

At a glance

  • Monitoring software for a vanadium redox-flow battery system.
  • Reads flow, temperature, pressure and charge/discharge state over CAN and RS485.
  • Configurable threshold-based fault alarms and protective logic.

Outcome

Automated inverter control enabled charge/discharge tests without continuous manual supervision, with fault alarms and protective logic.

The problem

Charge and discharge tests needed continuous manual supervision, and faults in flow, temperature or pressure had to be caught.

What I did

  • Developed monitoring software using CAN and RS485 data for flow, temperature, pressure and charge/discharge state.
  • Implemented configurable threshold-based fault alarms and protective logic.
  • Automated inverter control for charge and discharge tests.
