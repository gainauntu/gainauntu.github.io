Autonomous Water-Treatment Skimmer

Organization
Fluton Inc., with K-water
Period
2021–2023
My role
Sole software developer
Status
Field-tested with K-water engineers

Product context (external link)

Fluton's website shows the company's SweepRay surface drones in manufacturer photographs. They are product context, not photographs of the K-water test platform described on this page. SweepRay on Fluton's website

At a glance

  • An autonomous surface platform that runs repeatable cleaning and survey missions in water-treatment pools.
  • Pixhawk/PX4 and RTK-GPS for missions, with LiDAR-based collision avoidance near pool walls and facility structures.
  • AprilTag-guided docking on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano, followed by electromagnetic dock retention and wireless charging.

Outcome

K-water field testing demonstrated complete unattended cleaning, docking and charging cycles, including multi-hour autonomous operation.

The problem

For the platform to work without an operator, it had to repeat its missions reliably, keep clear of pool walls and facility structures, and dock and recharge by itself.

What I did

  • Sole software developer for the platform.
  • Implemented repeatable cleaning and survey missions using Pixhawk/PX4 and RTK-GPS.
  • Implemented LiDAR-based collision avoidance near pool walls and facility structures.
  • Implemented AprilTag-guided docking on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano and the post-alignment sequence that engages electromagnetic retention and wireless charging.
