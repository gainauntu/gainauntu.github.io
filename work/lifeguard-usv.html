Lifeguard Autonomous Surface Vehicle

Organization
Fluton Inc.
Period
2021–2023
My role
Perception, integration and simulation
Status
Simulation-tested before field trials

Product context (external link)

Fluton exhibited a lifeguard surface drone at RobotWorld 2021. The exhibition listing is product context; it does not show my own work or a real rescue. RobotWorld 2021 exhibition listing (Korea Association of Robot Industry)

At a glance

  • TensorFlow object-detection models fine-tuned to find people in water, deployed on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano.
  • Pose estimation on detected people: arm-raise angles flag possible distress postures and trigger alerts during survey missions.
  • LiDAR, ultrasonic and RealSense camera data integrated through ROS2 for obstacle avoidance.
  • Perception connected to vehicle behavior through Pixhawk/ArduPilot, QGroundControl and custom MAVLink interfaces.

Outcome

Possible distress postures were flagged during survey missions; manual and autonomous survey behavior was tested in simulation before field trials.

The problem

During survey missions the vessel had to notice a person who might be in distress and raise an alert, while avoiding obstacles around it.

What I did

  • Fine-tuned pretrained TensorFlow object-detection models on a project-specific dataset of people in water and deployed them on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano.
  • Applied MediaPipe pose estimation to detected persons and calculated arm-raise angles from pose landmarks.
  • Integrated LiDAR, ultrasonic and RealSense data through ROS2 for obstacle avoidance.
  • Connected perception results and vehicle behavior through Pixhawk/ArduPilot, QGroundControl and custom MAVLink interfaces.
  • Used ROS2/Gazebo simulation to test manual-control and autonomous-survey behavior before field trials.
